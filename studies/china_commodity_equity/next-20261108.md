# Commodity allocation, cash controls and contract accounting

[简体中文](next-20261108.zh-CN.md)

The commodity sleeve added historical return relative to holding more cash at approximately comparable volatility. It did not dominate cash on drawdown. A first individual-contract experiment and prospective macro capture are complete; neither establishes attainable futures returns or macro timing effectiveness.

## Cash and risk controls

A 756-session warmup on the previously audited CSI 300 total-return / Nanhua commodity daily panel moves the common evaluation sample to 2010-02-26 through 2025-12-31, 3,852 observations. Cash yield and fees are zero in every control. Monthly rebalancing uses fractional index sleeves.

| Comparison | CAGR | Annualized daily volatility | Maximum daily drawdown |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 100% equities | 4.44% | 21.65% | -46.06% |
| 80% equities / 20% commodities | 4.78% | 18.52% | -40.18% |
| 80% equities / 20% cash | 3.91% | 17.29% | -38.36% |
| Past-volatility stock/cash rule | 4.11% | 18.48% | -39.56% |
| Full-sample volatility cash diagnostic | 4.08% | 18.50% | -40.57% |

The past-volatility rule estimates the daily-rebalanced 80/20 model volatility divided by equity volatility from 756 completed daily returns. At each completed month-end it sets the next holding segment's stock allocation, capped between zero and one. Closing trades are idealized. The 191 decision weights range from 82.51% to 88.58%. Realized volatility differs from the commodity portfolio by -0.22% relative; future risk need not match exactly.

The full-sample diagnostic uses hindsight and a constant 85.56% stock target. It is not a trading rule or exact volatility match after drift. No drawdown target was optimized. Lower stock exposure explains part of the risk reduction. The commodity sleeve has higher CAGR here but larger drawdown than both the fixed cash control and the past-volatility cash rule. No out-of-sample superiority is established.

## One contract-level trial

Use DCE soymeal, ten tonnes per lot, CNY 1 million initial capital, zero cash interest. This is a retrospective rule chosen for this exploration, not a contemporaneously preregistered strategy.

- Enter M2505 at the 2024-12-31 settlement mark.
- Roll to M2509 on 2025-04-01 and M2601 on 2025-08-01.
- Exit on 2025-12-31 before the next delivery month.

Integer lots use prior-session equity and the new contract's prior settlement. Existing holdings earn their own settlement-to-settlement P&L; the contract price gap creates no profit. Recorded exchange fees are CNY 1.5 per traded lot. Initial entry, both roll legs and final exit are charged. Margin uses recorded daily speculative rates plus five percentage points; it is reserved, not deducted again from equity. No insufficiency occurred in these scenarios.

| Adverse ticks per traded lot | Exchange fees | Slippage | Account return | Maximum daily drawdown | Maximum margin / equity |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0 | CNY 318 | CNY 0 | 7.58% | -9.25% | 14.97% |
| 1 | CNY 318 | CNY 2,120 | 7.37% | -9.39% | 14.97% |
| 2 | CNY 318 | CNY 4,240 | 7.16% | -9.52% | 14.98% |

There are 212 traded lots. Independent segment endpoints give CNY 76,110 gross P&L and reconcile all terminal values exactly. Return includes entry costs; drawdown includes pre-entry capital. The Nanhua soymeal index gained 2.25% in the same calendar year, with different roll and weight rules. The gap is not tracking error or investable alpha, and this trial does not reconstruct the composite index.

Missing roll dates, missing marks, settlement disagreements and percentage fees of unverified units fail closed.

Settlement is not an executable quote. Broker fees, price limits, depth, order participation and intraday margin calls remain unverified. The margin buffer is an assumption. This is accounting only; production execution belongs to `quant-platform`.
