Fixed cost scenarios are 0, 10 and 50 bp per side. The browser also compares retrospective adjusted NAVs with adjusted market prices. There are no real orders, observed trades or completed forward windows. NAV premiums and macro labels are not timing signals.

The [protocol](paper-protocol-v1.json) and [registration](paper-registration-20261108.json) are hash-bound. Changes require a new protocol ID. Append-only observation ingestion accepts hash-bound normalized owner growth inputs from one provider vintage, rejects marks before the eligible close, missing sessions, rewrites and reversed recording chronology. It does not acquire prices or authenticate supplier statements. Automated paper-price delivery is not installed. The next real step is to supply the first eligible observation after the future close; no past performance is relabeled as forward evidence. Review horizons are 12/36/60 months; early observations remain descriptive.
