# First-round commodity and equity findings

[Chinese version](findings-20261107.zh-CN.md)

Commodity outperformance depends strongly on the window. From December 29, 2006 to December 31, 2025, CSI 300 total return compounded faster than the Nanhua commodity index. The predeclared 80/20 monthly mix reduced losses and volatility in this sample, but its gross annualized return advantage was small. This is descriptive index research, with 4,619 common daily marks and 229 month-end marks; it is not an implemented futures account.

Ten-year loss-window fractions were 6.42% for equities and 11.93% for commodities, based on 109 overlapping completed windows. These historical frequencies do not estimate future probabilities. Neither asset made long holding periods uniformly safe.

For 80/20, annual rebalancing produced 7.00% CAGR and a 64.67% maximum drawdown; monthly rebalancing produced 6.54% and 65.91%. A hypothetical 100-bp annual wealth drag reduces the monthly mix CAGR to 5.48%. These drags are not calibrated costs. On equal drag assumptions the stock-only comparison must receive the same drag. No observed optimal weight or rebalance frequency is recommended.

Monthly stock/commodity correlation was 0.342. Across 99 negative-equity months, commodities averaged −1.11% and rose in only 33.33% of months. Diversification reduced some portfolio risk; commodities did not provide a dependable crash hedge.

## Evidence limits and next work

The supplier's observed composite, industrial, agriculture and energy histories begin January 4, 2006, rather than the index's 2004 base. Two SHFE-only sessions, January 26–27, 2006, are missing from these series and remain empty on narrow requery. The original full-history audit is retained. The explicitly shorter baseline starts at the first year-end after that gap, without filling prices. CSI All Share total return also lacks August 2, 2011, its listed launch date in the frozen catalog; earlier history is backfilled. Its full daily comparison remains blocked. CSI 1000 total return passed the shorter baseline checks.

Seven December 31, 2025 index closes match the original publisher report at its reported precision. That point check does not certify the complete series. The report's ARR column is annualized; its 6.56% composite figure is not assumed to equal the 5.40% calendar endpoint return. Independent annual-return reconciliation remains open.

The 2025 methodology describes an eight-session roll in its introduction and five daily 20% transfers in its detailed rules. Historical methodology versions, collateral interest and fees are unresolved. Do not add a second roll-return adjustment to these published index levels. Fully collateralized futures, ETFs and CTA net values require separate evidence and accounting.

Next priorities are the unresolved source/calendar gaps, original annual-return reconciliation, historical methodology versions, contract-level implementation, and macro regimes using actual release vintages. Inventory, warehouse receipts and macro prediction have not been tested. No statistical significance, forward expected return, or personal allocation recommendation is claimed.
