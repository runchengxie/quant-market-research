## Predeclared allocation rules

All comparisons use January 2007–December 2025, 227 returns. Equity-only, stock/bond 60/40 and stock/bond/gold equal weights were fixed before results. Multi-asset rules are buy-and-hold, every twelve elapsed months, or a 5-percentage-point absolute target deviation. Returns precede month-end trades; trades affect later holding. References are CSI 300/1000 total-return histories, not necessarily the fund contract's price-index benchmark. NAV already reflects internal expenses; management fees are not deducted again. Differences are not pure fee estimates; positive gaps are not automatically manager alpha or product recommendations.

Market/reference relative wealth is reconciled multiplicatively into NAV/reference, raw closing-price/unit-NAV premium change, and the residual between market and NAV distribution-adjustment bases. For 512100 the residual wealth ratio is approximately 0.99093, so premium changes alone do not explain the market/NAV difference.

The long-window positive gap requires annual reconciliation. Selected issuer checks are consistent: calculated 2025 adjusted-NAV growth is 29.2341% and five-year growth 21.9524%, matching disclosed 29.23%/21.95% at report precision. This neither verifies the complete 2017–2025 history nor explains economic causality. [Issuer annual report, sections 3.1 and 3.2.1](https://www.sse.com.cn/disclosure/fund/announcement/c/new/2026-03-31/512100_20260331_H7TX.pdf)

All 108 month-end marks match their valuation dates. 159919/512100 each have one missing daily market session, not filled or zeroed; full daily execution coverage is not certified.
