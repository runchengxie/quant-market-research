# Uncertainty, spending and investability

The small-cap conclusion depends on the sampled history and era; rebalancing and spending outcomes also depend on return order. Gold can fall during equity stress, funded cash changes exposure as well as sale timing, and published index history should be distinguished from back-calculated history. These are historical experiments through December 2025, not future loss probabilities or prospective observations.

## Fixed experiments and findings

**Paired uncertainty.** Circular blocks of 6/12/24 months, 1,000 draws each and a fixed seed preserve same-month cross-asset pairing. The sample is 252 monthly small/large returns and 227 monthly equity/bond/gold returns. Each sampled sequence recomputes fixed annual 60/40 rebalancing and buy-and-hold. The 2.5th–97.5th percentile ranges for small/large relative CAGR are −6.37% to 9.52%, −6.45% to 9.03%, and −6.66% to 9.54%. Annual-minus-hold CAGR differences range from −4.58 to 2.04, −4.49 to 2.57 and −2.46 to 2.97 percentage points. Drawdown differences also span zero; positive differences mean shallower rebalanced drawdowns. Costs are excluded.

These ranges condition on observed history and block assumptions, including artificial block joins and circular wrapping. They are not multiple-testing-adjusted inference, future confidence guarantees or independent validation. Fixed-era small/large relative CAGR is 3.62%, 5.27%, −5.79%, 1.85% for 2005–09, 2010–14, 2015–19, 2020–25. The multi-asset first era has only 35 returns, February 2007–December 2009. Structural change and missing tail events are not cured by more resampling.

**Spending.** Initial real capital 100, annual real withdrawals 0/2/4/6 paid monthly with CPI indexing, zero-interest funded cash buffers 0/12/24 months, and a one-off real demand of 20 after 12 or 60 months (or none). Three portfolios and these rules produce 108 fixed scenarios, each with 108 overlapping complete ten-year windows. Cash reduces initial risky capital, is spent first and is never refilled automatically. Remaining demands sell the sleeve pro rata. Returns precede month-end demands; annual rebalancing resets at every formation. No taxes, transaction frictions or leverage are assumed. Buffers cover recurring spending, not a separate reserve for the one-off shock.

For annual real spending 4 and a month-12 shock of 20, equity-only shortfalls occur in 6/108 windows without cash and 4/108 with a 24-month buffer. Both fixed 60/40 and equal stock/bond/gold have 0/108 in that particular experiment. Equity median real sales during sleeve drawdown fall from 56.33 to 49.22, while median terminal real capital changes from 65.65 to 65.24. Failed accounts stay in denominators; exact exhaustion after all demands is satisfied is not a shortfall. These histories cannot establish a safe lifetime withdrawal rate. The [Society of Actuaries' evaluation primer](https://www.soa.org/globalassets/assets/files/resources/research-report/2023/ret-income-strat-de.pdf) discusses return sequencing and withdrawals as interacting risks.

**Stress.** Contemporaneous masks are equity monthly return ≤−5%, bond return ≤−1%, and trailing 12-month CPI inflation ≥3%. They are descriptive, not trading signals or causal regimes. Correlations need at least 12 observations and nonzero variance. Among 42 equity-stress months, average bond return is +0.72% and gold −0.69%; equity and gold are both negative in 20/42 (47.62%), equity and bonds in 9/42 (21.43%). Equity/bond correlation is −0.15 within that mask and −0.24 over all 227 months. These cannot be equated to the report's differently sampled annual correlations. CPI rounding affects near-threshold classification.

Fixed 2008/2015/2020/2022 calendar intervals track recovery from the interval's maximum-drawdown peak; equal peaks use the latest pre-trough peak. Unrecovered paths are right-censored. The 2015 equity interval remains unrecovered after 127 observed months. The configured service denied the documented [`yc_cb` yield API](https://tushare.pro/wctapi/documents/201.md) with code40203; no actual rate-rise mask is admitted. Bond losses are not labeled rate increases. [ChinaBond](https://yield.chinabond.com.cn/cbweb-pbc-web/pbc/more?locale=cn_zh) is an identified source, not a verified complete export. [BIS research](https://www.bis.org/publications/correlation-equity-and-bond-returns) motivates testing changing correlations; it does not substitute for Chinese evidence.

**Investability.** Official CSI factsheets establish price-family publication dates: [All Share](https://oss-ch.csindex.com.cn/static/html/csindex/public/uploads/indices/detail/files/zh_CN/000985factsheet.pdf) August2,2011; [CSI300](https://oss-ch.csindex.com.cn/static/html/csindex/public/uploads/indices/detail/files/zh_CN/000300factsheet.pdf) April8,2005; [CSI1000](https://oss-ch.csindex.com.cn/static/html/csindex/public/uploads/indices/detail/files/zh_CN/000852factsheet.pdf) October17,2014. The first complete month after launch through December2025 gives total-return-reference CAGRs of 5.76%, 10.11%, 2.72%, respectively. CSI1000 relative CAGR versus CSI300 on the identical post-launch interval is −4.89%. These date families do not certify original total-return-series vintages. Different eras cannot prove that back-calculation caused the differences; pre-launch CSI300 has just three months and annualization is particularly fragile.

Additional ETF API probes leave a November30,2012 NAV gap for510300 and market-price gap for159919, including targeted retries. Both retain verified 2017–25 coverage.512100 extends to November30,2016 with109 returns: market proxy CAGR1.35%, or1.24% after hypothetical50bp each side. Differing intervals are not rankings. Entry costs scale invested shares by1/(1+c), exit costs by1−c. NAV already embeds internal expenses; current fees are not applied retrospectively. Spread, taxes, market impact, complete daily coverage and executable closing quotes remain unverified. Dividend API responses ignored requested date bounds and were retained but not summed as cash receipts. Earlier NAV discrepancies remain in the prior closure record.

**Conclusion register.** Seven stable claims retain evidence stage, scope, supporting files, review triggers and open questions in [conclusion-register.json](conclusion-register.json). Sign changes, stress joint losses, source gaps and missing pre-entry proof trigger review. Blocked evidence does not upgrade automatically. The prospective protocol remains unadmitted with zero completed windows.

## Reproduction and boundaries

Use `scripts/run_asset_decision_study.py --help` with explicit first-round inputs, first-round owner task, robustness owner, reviewed follow-up run and new owner task; choose a new external output directory. No calls fetch data or mutate shared aliases. Source hashes bind first-round inputs, reviewed follow-up results, official PDF snapshots, supplier responses and calculation code. Full per-window spending records and monthly product marks stay external. `project_asset_decision_summary.py` exposes reviewed aggregates only; the release manifest locks both public evidence and the conclusion register. The Chinese companion gives additional tables and interpretation. No historical experiment is relabeled as prospective evidence.
