# Long-horizon returns: first replication and data-access findings

[中文完整记录](findings-20261031.zh-CN.md)

**Three equity price/total-return comparisons and a monthly equity/gold/treasury diversification study are complete.** Independent earnings explanations and actual distribution/withdrawal accounts remain unsupported. An accessible API does not resolve historical vintages or cash entitlements.

Observations end on 2025-12-31; retrieval was on 2026-10-31. This is retrospective descriptive evidence, including pre-launch index backfills. Raw responses remain under the data owner's task directory; frozen research inputs and full runs remain under the research data root at `tasks/asset-returns-20261031/`. Preparation revision: `81e5e4f`; calculation revision: `e718b5b`. Results also record input, metadata and calculation-file hashes.

## Return reconciliation

All equity comparisons use 253 monthly levels from 2004-12-31 to 2025-12-31 (252 return intervals).

| Index | Price CAGR | Total-return CAGR | Geometric distribution residual |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| CSI All Share | 8.84% | 10.40% | 1.43% |
| CSI 300 | 7.57% | 9.58% | 1.87% |
| CSI 1000 | 10.14% | 10.87% | 0.67% |

The residual is `(1+total CAGR)/(1+price CAGR)-1`, not cash received. Components compound rather than add.

SBBI reports 9.71% for CSI 300 after separately estimating and correcting 2005 dividend reinvestment. This study preserves the unmodified TuShare H00300.CSI history. Multiplying initial-year wealth by the report's 1.026 adjustment produces approximately 9.71% over 21 years, numerically explaining the gap without independently certifying that dividend estimate. [SBBI estimation notes](https://youzhiyouxing.cn/sbbi2025/estimated-data/)

## Ten-year holding risk

Each index has 133 complete, overlapping 120-month windows and 120 entry dates without ten years of follow-up.

| Total-return index | Nominal loss windows | Purchasing-power loss windows | Worst nominal CAGR |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| CSI All Share | 3/133 (2.26%) | 17/133 (12.78%) | -2.29% |
| CSI 300 | 7/133 (5.26%) | 17/133 (12.78%) | -1.99% |
| CSI 1000 | 11/133 (8.27%) | 22/133 (16.54%) | -6.59% |

These are historical frequencies, not future loss probabilities. Overlapping windows are not independent. Purchasing power chains rounded national monthly CPI changes; it is approximate and differs from annual-average inflation. Monthly observations miss intramonth drawdowns and recovery touches. Unrecovered entries remain censored, including the final entry with no follow-up.

## Diversification when equities decline

The exact common window is 2007-01-31 to 2025-12-31, with 227 monthly returns. Earlier treasury history is not estimated.

| Monthly statistic | Au99.99 gold | 7–10-year treasury wealth index |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Correlation with CSI All Share total return | 0.125 | -0.240 |
| Positive fraction in 101 negative-equity months | 58.42% | 73.27% |
| Average return in those months | 0.54% | 0.48% |
| Worst return in those months | -14.92% | -2.21% |

Different frequency and dates prevent direct comparison with SBBI's annual correlation matrix. An equally weighted, idealized simultaneous month-end mix has 8.03% gross CAGR and -32.19% month-end maximum drawdown. Assumed annual wealth drags of 50/100 bp reduce CAGR to 7.49%/6.95%. These are fixed sensitivities, not calibrated trading costs or executable account performance. Gold uses spot prices without custody, delivery or investment-product expenses. Weights were not optimized to these results.

Neither leg has calibrated execution costs. CSI 1000 is not a microcap strategy. Existing execution, capacity and exit evidence retains its authority.

## Supplier access and remaining evidence

- TuShare returned all six equity histories and gold with 253 verified common final-session marks. Some daily gaps remain outside this endpoint-only study.
- `cn_cpi` returned 253 months with nonmissing national monthly changes. Original publication vintages are unverified.
- `dividend` returned 54 records for a sample stock, including proposal status and record/ex/payment dates. These are not necessarily 54 implemented distributions.
- `index_weight` returned 300 CSI 300 constituent rows for January 2025: one successful month does not establish complete historical constituents or announcement availability.
- `fx_daily` returned 26 October 2025 USDCNH.FXCM rows. CNH is not CNY; GMT dates, dealer bid/ask marks and valuation time require alignment before global portfolio use.
- `yc_cb` returned code 40203; official documentation requires independent permission. A yield curve is not a bond total-return series.
- ChinaBond's public routes used by AKShare returned 4,954 observations each for 7–10-year treasury wealth/net-price indices and 6,208 composite-duration observations. Only the treasury wealth series enters this portfolio; composite duration is not treasury duration.
- `index_basic` returned 8,000 rows, reaching its cap. It confirms names of returned codes, not a complete catalog.

Only reviewed derived aggregates are published. Successful API access does not certify complete history, economic semantics or redistribution rights; bulk and commercial redistribution remain unverified.

The earnings identity diagnostic exists, but independent financial scope, shares, constituent histories and original vintages remain necessary. Existing `revision_safe=false` inputs cannot become strict PIT evidence here. Monthly cash scenarios are tested but actual cash accounts require an entitlement/payment ledger; total-minus-price differences are never invented cash distributions. Bond wealth/net-price differences require accrued-interest and reinvestment reconciliation before being called coupons. Overseas ETF distributions and FX alignment remain incomplete. Actual microcap net-return claims remain constrained by the existing execution study.
