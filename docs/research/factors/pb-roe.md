# PB and ROE: comparing valuation and profitability

[Chinese version](pb-roe.zh-CN.md)

This page preserves the public summary of historical PB/ROE experiments and their method notes. The unified research entry now lives in the [PB/ROE study at Quant Factor Observatory](https://runchengxie.github.io/quant-factor-observatory/studies/pb-roe).

**Plain-language takeaway:** In this historical comparison using the same candidate stocks, adding static ROE produced lower returns and information ratios than ranking by PB alone. This finding applies to this sample and these rules; it does not show that ROE is generally ineffective. The “PB-only” group still required ROE data to be available.

**Annualized excess return** is the annualized return above the stated benchmark. **Maximum drawdown** is the largest fall from a portfolio high to a later low. The **information ratio** summarizes excess return relative to its variability; none of these measures alone proves that a result is reliable or tradable.

The published numbers are historical derived results through 2026-08-31. They were checked against source CSV files, run manifests, and audit records. This site does not independently rerun the complete history and does not present the comparison as a tradable strategy.

Interpretation must distinguish valuation, profitability, portfolio construction, and execution assumptions. Any conclusion is limited by the source data's point-in-time availability and the documented sample definition.
