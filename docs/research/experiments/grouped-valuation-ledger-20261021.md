# Grouped valuation: constrained adjusted-unit ledger checkpoint

This is an unpublished research diagnostic, not a raw-share account or executable performance claim. Financial and reconstructed ST histories remain `revision_safe=false`. Original signal membership is frozen before looking at future prices. The existing 36 monthly cohorts and all five quintiles are preserved.

## Accounting contract, fixed before the pilot

1. Each quintile starts with 1,000,000 units of scenario cash. At a rebalance, all older lots receive irrevocable exit instructions. Failed exits remain held and are attempted on every following exchange session, including across later rebalances. A new cohort splits only the cash actually available after that day's successful exits equally across its complete original membership. This deliberately high-turnover cohort policy differs from a netted target-weight rebalance. Retained old lots remain additional exposure and are never counted as cash.
2. Entry is one attempt at the next session's close; a failed entry expires and its allocation stays cash until the next cohort. No redistribution among successful entrants. If a name still has a pending old exit, its new entry also expires. The final cohort endpoint issues liquidation instructions; a fixed 20-session tail attempts exits without opening new positions.
3. The main daily assumption refuses a buy on any upper-limit touch (`high >= up_limit`) and a sale on any lower-limit touch (`low <= down_limit`), as well as absent/invalid prices, zero amount, suspension, missing flags or limits. The sensitivity refuses close-limit flags only. Neither daily OHLC nor positive volume proves that an order would fill. Every other eligible order is hypothetically filled at adjusted close. No queue, impact, capacity, lot-size or auction model is claimed. Selling and reinvesting exact proceeds at the same close additionally assumes price knowledge and within-close funding order; real closing-order feasibility is unproven.
4. Cash debit/credit uses hypothetical adjusted-price units and proportional costs, with 0/10/30 bps per side sensitivity. Costs are an all-in parameter, not a historical statutory fee/tax reconstruction. A buy's budget includes costs; sales incur costs once, only upon fill. Units are fractional research units, never called legal shares. Same-session resale of new units is excluded by the cohort order of operations.
5. Mark all held units every session. A valid same-day adjusted close may mark a suspended/limit-blocked holding without permitting a trade. Missing marks remain unknown. `strict_nav` is absent if any held lot lacks an acceptable quote or has reached its retrospective delisting date. `zero_unresolved_nav` is a disclosed impairment sensitivity, retaining units and generating no cash. Store the last observed mark and its date for audit, never use a future mark or silently fill the strict series. A delisting date from the retained master is retrospective metadata, not proof of announcement-time availability; no recovery, proceeds or cancellation of units is invented.
6. Adjusted-unit mode admits no separate dividend or stock distribution postings. Actual raw-share mode fails closed until action completeness, record/ex/payment/listing dates, event conflicts, entitlement/tax rules, adjustment reconciliation and terminal recovery evidence are certified. Existing issuer dividend rows do not prove all-event completeness or investor cash receipt. Test the refusal explicitly.

## Outputs and verification

Every order attempt has stable lot/cohort identifiers, request and attempt dates, side, original target weight, budget, units, price, fee, filled status and failure reason. Daily holdings preserve units, exit request, mark source, last quote and unresolved reason. Daily cash/NAV rows retain cash, known marked value, unresolved units/count, pending exits and cumulative costs. A separate verifier replays filled orders to reconcile units, fees and cash against every daily snapshot, checks the NAV identity, and rejects unsupported ledger cash/unit changes or negative cash. It does not independently prove market fill eligibility; a separate source-backed SQL audit checks saved hypothetical fills against frozen daily inputs, still without queue execution proof.

First test blocked buys, limit exits, cross-rebalance carries, missing quotes, later quote recovery, terminal assets, costs, final liquidation, invalid input, action double counting and causality. Then a small deterministic actual-data pilot; only after it passes run the original 36-period PB and deducted-PE all-market baskets. Do not filter difficult future members. ST subsets still have small-sample warnings and are not enlarged by relaxing the pre-existing signal threshold.

Raw source data, identities and runtime output stay outside Git. Receipts bind source/code hashes and explicit inputs, prohibit overwriting any output, and preserve earlier run01/review02/annual_minima01. No publication, push, merge or deployment is part of this checkpoint.
