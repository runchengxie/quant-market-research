# Research closeout status

[Chinese version](research-closeout-status.zh-CN.md)

This page records the current state of public research cleanup, completed organization work, and remaining data or manual verification tasks. It describes evidence status and does not imply that a strategy is ready for live trading.

The public site separates reviewed derived outputs from raw provider data and complete run outputs. Historical results retain their source dates and methodology boundaries. A result remains pending when the source, point-in-time availability, execution assumptions, or independent audit is incomplete.

See the individual factor pages for the current evidence boundary. Ownership and publication rules are maintained in the repository's internal research documentation.

The status labels have specific meanings: `exploration` means the conclusion may change as checks continue; `incomplete` means important data or verification is still missing; `derived` means a result was calculated from other data and is not automatically independently validated. **Point-in-time (PIT) data** is information available at the date being studied. **ADV** is average trading value over a stated period, used as a rough guide to trading capacity.
