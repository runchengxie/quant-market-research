# Cash-flow report reading design

The user asks for plain Chinese explanations, richer charts and complete evidence retention. Keep the Astro site, its English switch and immutable source downloads. Rewrite the reading layer in a locale catalog. Lead with available-snapshot calibration and the limitations of the 80-month research portfolios. Explain correlation, weighting, turnover and point-in-time inputs beside their use.

Use a dated evidence coverage list rather than a continuous timeline that could imply unavailable weights or uninterrupted coverage. Show exact correlations as coefficients, never success rates. Replace small SVG bars with responsive grouped horizontal bars, zero baselines, direct values and accessible full tables. Keep both original chart series and every category. Preserve four research steps, six trust checks, the decision change, next task and provenance.

A copy-only edit would leave the mobile charts difficult to read. A new interactive dashboard would add complexity without additional evidence. Use the existing report with better reading order and directly labeled charts.

Validate source hashes, reader-visible facts, language switching, punctuation, mobile geometry and chart values. Review the finished copy and screenshots before PR integration.
