# Complete research topics for new readers

The user chose multiple topics and asked to keep content expanded, with the remaining reading improvements delegated to the implementer. Keep the existing Astro/ECharts stack and bilingual catalogs.

## Approach

Create three asset topics alongside the original cash-flow topic. Two large pages would still combine unrelated questions. A separate page for every chart would fragment the explanation. Three asset pages keep connected evidence together.

- `/research/asset-returns/` retains all long-return evidence, entry/exit sensitivity, relative waiting and paired uncertainty.
- `/research/asset-allocation/` retains allocation rules, cash spending, stressed co-movement and stress recovery events.
- `/research/asset-implementation/` retains ETF reconciliation, constituent/financial coverage, launch history, closure, prospective limits and the conclusion register.
- `/research/cashflow/` retains its original index performance, recovery and replication research, with links to the asset topics.

All original prose, evidence rows, source links and downloads remain represented. Additional plain-language explanations define holding windows, real money, relative returns and rebalancing near their first use. Detailed methods and evidence tables are expanded by default, and successful chart loading never folds a table. Native disclosures may remain open and reader-controlled. No statistical calculations, data bytes, old archived editions or research stages change.

Each asset page uses a short introduction, visible sample/boundary notes, a section directory, full evidence and links to neighboring topics. Text lines are narrower than figures and tables. Chart controls stay beside the relevant graph. English remains the default with a persistent explicit language switch. Cash-flow legacy anchors link/redirect to the new owning topics while preserving query parameters; no-JavaScript readers receive visible links. Discovery and search include the new topics.

## Acceptance

All ten charts occur once across the three asset pages. Full evidence remains readable with scripts disabled. Existing selected-row tables and controls retain exact values. All defaults stay expanded after lazy chart loading, locale and theme changes. Both languages fit mobile and desktop. Every former research block has an explicit destination, checked by a migration coverage record. Legacy URLs remain useful. Required local and remote checks pass before the authorized merge and Pages publication.
