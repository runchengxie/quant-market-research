# Decision-risk extension to the asset-return study

Authorization: the owner explicitly requested all five proposed follow-ups, with implementation details delegated in the conversation. Existing Python/pandas and Astro architecture retained; no repeated design approval is needed.

## Approach and scope

Extend the frozen descriptive study with finite sensitivity experiments, rather than fitting a return forecast or introducing a general execution engine. A full portfolio backtester belongs to the provider platform; this task uses explicitly labeled study accounting. Monthly history ends in 2025. It is not a prospective outcome. Original financial/ETF reconciliation gaps remain visible.

1. **Uncertainty:** paired circular moving-block resampling of monthly asset returns (blocks 6/12/24 months, 1,000 draws, fixed seed), full-length sequences, relative small/large geometric CAGR and equity/bond/gold correlation. Recompute fixed 60/40 annual-versus-hold CAGR and drawdown differences on identical sampled sequences. Report 2.5/97.5 percentile resampling ranges and sign fractions as conditional sensitivities, never probabilities about future returns. Also compare fixed nonoverlapping calendar eras, keeping partial eras explicit.
2. **Spending:** normalize initial real capital to 100. Compare stocks, fixed annual 60/40, equal stock/bond/gold; initial real annual withdrawals 0/2/4/6, cash buffers 0/12/24 months, and a real 20 one-off demand after 12 or 60 months. Full 120-month rolling windows only. Asset returns occur before CPI-indexed month-end demands; cash earns zero nominal interest, starts funded by reducing risky holdings, no automatic refill. Spend cash first then sell risky holdings pro rata. Keep unmet demand, first shortfall, drawdown-time sales and terminal real capital; never drop failed accounts. Overlapping window fractions are historical descriptions, not failure probabilities. Isolate sequence effects by reversing an explicit return sequence in tests, not changing the original observations.
3. **Stress:** contemporaneous descriptive masks: equity monthly return <=-5%, bond monthly return <=-1%, trailing 12-month CPI inflation >=3%, and full sample. Not policy signals; no trading or causal inference from these labels. Show count, pairwise correlations (unavailable with <12 observations or zero variance), joint-negative frequencies, conditional companion returns, and recovery of fixed 2008/2015/2020/2022 calendar stress intervals with right-censoring.
4. **Investability:** verify official launch dates and split back-calculated/post-launch monthly histories using the first complete month after launch. Keep index publication distinct from total-return-series publication and ETF availability. Reuse frozen ETF market/NAV proxies for overlapping return gaps; add fixed hypothetical per-side 0/10/50bp entry/exit costs, never double-subtract NAV expenses or silently repair early mismatches. No account-executable or total cost certification.
5. **Conclusion register:** stable machine identifiers, evidence stages, exact scopes, supporting artifact locations, falsification/review triggers and unresolved data for each research claim. Labels cannot upgrade descriptive history to prospective evidence. All five workstreams appear in bilingual findings and existing topic page via reviewed aggregates and a hash lock.
