# Execution-cost evidence audit

Independent recomputation confirms all eight static side/size cells, the 306,483 valid and 4,912 one-sided saved books, lagged daily groups, 18 funded strategy ledgers and 80 synthetic order measurements. This does not certify the complete raw warehouse, historical publication availability or actual broker execution.

The audit found a missing TradingDay filter in the snapshot-path runner. Its saved input includes January 4, 15 and 29. Five of 72 v6 scenarios used foreign-day quotes in 17 fills. v7 filters January 4 explicitly and rejects mixed-date or mixed-security input. All 72 corrected paths and 234 fills were independently reconciled against same-day saved books. Limit-buy completions change from 8 to 7 of 12. Buy TWAP median shortfall changes from −14.95 to −7.63 bp, sell TWAP from 38.62 to 25.36 bp. These shortfalls include market moves and do not rank live algorithms.

Cost quantiles condition on sufficient displayed depth and pool valid security-minutes equally. Uncovered orders have missing costs. The 12 fixed-fill overlays contain 51–55 negative-cash sessions and remain unexecutable accounting comparisons. The 18 rerun scenarios have no negative cash. Synthetic measurements test software only.

Priority follow-up is actual orders, fills, contemporaneously received quotes and fee reports; original-warehouse earliest-minute selection and absent-security audits; predeclared normal/stress dates; and vendor event dictionaries plus cross-stream ordering. Real slippage, own impact and executable capacity remain unverified.
