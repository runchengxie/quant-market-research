# Quant Market Research

[Chinese version](index.zh-CN.md)

This site explains what public factor research has found so far, how the numbers were produced, and what is still uncertain. Start with a topic's plain-language takeaway, then use the tables and method notes to check the details.

English and Chinese pages have separate stable URLs. Use the language link at the top of a page to switch versions, or share that page's URL directly.

## Factor research

- [Research closeout status](research-closeout-status.md): what has been checked, what remains open, and what each status means.
- [PB and ROE](research/factors/pb-roe.md): whether adding profitability information changed a historical valuation comparison.
- [Low turnover](research/factors/low-turnover.md): what low trading activity may reflect, and whether its historical return gap survived further checks.
- [Microcap](research/factors/microcap.md): how reconstructed small-stock portfolios behaved, including their large losses and long recoveries.
- [Microcap execution diagnostic](research/experiments/microcap-execution-diagnostic-20260928.md): what happened when a short historical sample was run through a simulated trading process.
- [Small-cap turnover history](research/factors/smallcap-turnover-history.md): how much the smallest stocks traded in an earlier data period.
- [Barra factor dictionary](research/factors/barra-factor-dictionary.md): plain definitions of the public style-factor measures and their source limits.
- [Barra source inventory](research/factors/barra-source-inventory.md): which supporting materials can be used in public research.

Historical research results are evidence for further investigation. They are not trading recommendations or claims of future returns.

## Where factor research is published

Use this site for independent market research projects and their reproducible methods, sample definitions, results, and limitations. The [Quant Factor Observatory](https://runchengxie.github.io/quant-factor-observatory/) owns factor catalog organization, standardized factor studies, publication status, and reviewed projections under its publication contract. When a topic appears on both sites, follow the source identity in its study page or publication manifest; link to the authoritative material rather than copying it. Private strategy research remains in `quant-research`; publication there requires a separate review and redacted projection.
